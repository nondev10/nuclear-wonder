Pu-239 CANDU Breeder - Blueprint

Sodium-cooled, heavy-water moderated, CANDU-style heterogeneous net breeder. Scheme 2 reference case, ~50 MWth / ~5 kg/yr Pu-239.

Core Cross-Section

Hot sodium leg is accent-colored. The calandria honeycomb grid and moderator pool occupy the central section; the two-leg sodium loop routes out the right wall through the S-HX and pump back into the left wall.

Scheme 1 (TRIGA/CNA heavy-water pool, conversion) ~$235k · Scheme 2 (CANDU-type heavy-water breeder, net-breeding) ~$13.0M. Relative ratio scheme_1/scheme_2 ≈ 0.018 (≈55× cheaper). In Scheme 2, heavy-water tonnage is $2.2M of $13M ≈ 17% of capex, inside the published 11–20% CANDU heavy-water share.

Verdict: Pick Scheme 2 — the CANDU-type heavy-water breeder — because at ~5 kg/yr Pu-239 scale it is the only net-breeding topology that meets the production target; Scheme 1 is a converter and caps out below the required Pu-239 yield. The ~55× cost gap is not "meaningful" when the alternative simply cannot meet spec.
