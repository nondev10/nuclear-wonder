Fission Criticality Blueprint educational physics model

Interactive what-if for the nuclear physics of a fissile sphere: k-eff vs. radius, chain-reaction growth, energy release, and neutron/gamma leakage. The 2-group diffusion engine is calibrated to the *measured* Los Alamos critical masses (Hansen & Paxton, LA-4208: U-235 51.94 kg, Pu-239 17.02 kg, U-233 16.53 kg) and grounded in the Terrell MCNP leakage spectra (LA-11807). Criticality + kinetics + energy layer only — not a device specification.

Criticality & Leakage Diagram

k < 1 (M < M_crit): sub-critical — neutrons leak faster than they reproduce, the chain reaction settles to a steady sub-critical source. k = 1 (M = M_crit): critical. k > 1 (M > M_crit): prompt super-critical — the population grows on the ~140 ns generation time.

Device Fission Transient — gun / implosion assembly + kinetics + disassembly · educational

The device layer turns the bare-sphere engine into a time-dependent bomb: a sub-critical assembly (gun-assembly U-235, or implosion Pu-239) that is neutron-free during assembly, then the initiator fires at t₀ and the chain runs for a few prompt generation times before the hot core disassembles and the burst self-terminates. Grounded in the Nth Country fission-weapon design report (assembly time ≫ fission chain time). Device timescales are labeled order-of-magnitude estimates; the k-eff / criticality underneath is the calibrated engine.
